About Me

From the lab bench to the command line.

My work sits at the intersection of food science and applied technology.

My background

I trained and have worked in food science and technology, with hands-on experience in product formulation, food processing, food safety systems, and research supervision. I've consulted on HACCP implementation, taught food safety principles, and supported thesis and manuscript development for graduate researchers. My work has a particular focus on legumes and climate-smart crops, exploring how they can be processed into safe, marketable, nutritious products.

Why I build software tools

Over the past few years I've been building AI-enabled tools and web applications, using Firebase, Python, and agentic AI workflows, to solve the same problems I used to solve with spreadsheets and paper checklists: tracking compliance, managing training records, analyzing research data, and automating repetitive food safety documentation. This portfolio brings both sides of my work together.
