About Me

From the lab bench to the command line.

My work sits at the unique intersection of food science, regulatory compliance, and software engineering.

My Background & Expertise

I trained and have worked in food science and technology, with hands-on experience in product formulation, food processing, food safety management systems (FSMS), and research supervision. I've consulted on HACCP implementation, taught food safety principles, and supported thesis and manuscript development for graduate researchers.

My research and consulting particularly emphasize legumes and climate-smart crops, exploring how raw agricultural materials can be transformed into safe, shelf-stable, and highly nutritious food products.

Why I Build Software Tools

Over the past few years, I have been engineering AI-enabled tools and full-stack web applications using Firebase, Python, and agentic AI workflows. My goal is to replace slow, error-prone manual spreadsheets and paper checklists with intelligent systems.

From automated HACCP verification to real-time compliance tracking and research data analytics, these software applications bring structure, speed, and accuracy to food safety and scientific teams.
